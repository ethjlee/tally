Backup & restore

One tap opens the share sheet — choose Save to Files to drop a dated backup into iCloud Drive. Do this regularly; it's your only copy off this device.
Restore replaces your ledger. Merge adds anything new without touching your existing items or settings — use it to combine a backup from another device.
Complete CSV backup
